FIELD FILE No. 01 — RURAL HERITAGE WITHOUT BORDERS

Do young people still feel ownership of their own village's heritage?

Before a single workshop was designed, Asociația Vechea Bucovină asked. Between May and August 2026 the Romanian NGO ran a survey through its own Rural Heritage Data Hub, reaching young people in rural and small-town communities across six partner countries. What came back shaped every part of this project.
